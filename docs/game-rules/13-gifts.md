Triggered Gifts
A VERY RARE subset of permanent Gifts are triggered Gifts, which attach the user until
the trigger occurs. When their ability triggers, they are targeting the creature aﬀected by
the gift. Therefore a Gift of the Porcupine will not be usable by someone wearing a Heart
of Midnight, nor will it transfer as damage to someone wearing one, though it will trigger
(and have no eﬀect).
